10th Science (All Chapters)

CH 1 - CH 13 COMPLETE SCIENCE
Ch 1: Chemical Reactions & Equations

Equations represent chemical changes.

2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO (Combination Reaction)
Ch 2: Acids, Bases and Salts
pH Scale: Acid < 7 | Neutral = 7 | Base > 7

Bleaching Powder ($CaOCl_2$), Baking Soda ($NaHCO_3$), Washing Soda ($Na_2CO_3\cdot10H_2O$), POP ($CaSO_4\cdot\frac{1}{2}H_2O$).

Ch 3: Metals and Non-metals

Metals form basic oxides, are ionic with high melting points. Non-metals form acidic/neutral covalent bonds.

Ch 4: Carbon and its Compounds

Tetravalency and catenation properties. Saturated vs Unsaturated Hydrocarbons. Homologous series.

Ch 5: Life Processes

Vital pathways: Autotrophic/Heterotrophic Nutrition, Aerobic/Anaerobic Respiration, Heart Pumping, Nephron Excretion.

Ch 6: Control and Coordination

Nervous system (Reflex Arc, Brain layout) and Endocrine system hormones (Insulin, Thyroxine, Growth Hormone).

Ch 7: How do Organisms Reproduce?

Asexual modes (Fission, Budding, Regeneration) & Sexual structure in flowering plants and human systems.

Ch 8: Heredity

Mendel's monohybrid (3:1) and dihybrid (9:3:3:1) crosses. Sex determination parameters: XX (Girl), XY (Boy).

Ch 9: Light - Reflection & Refraction
Mirror: 1/f = 1/v + 1/u | Lens: 1/f = 1/v - 1/u | Power: P = 1/f(m)
Ch 10: Human Eye & Colorful World

Myopia (corrected by Concave lens), Hypermetropia (Convex lens), Prism dispersion, and Scattering rules.

Ch 11: Electricity
Ohm's Law: V = IR | Series: Rs = R1+R2 | Parallel: 1/Rp = 1/R1 + 1/R2 | Heat: H = I²Rt
Ch 12: Magnetic Effects of Current

Right-Hand Thumb Rule, Solenoid fields, Fleming's Left-Hand rule for magnetic forces on current carriers.

Ch 13: Our Environment

Food chains, Biodegradable waste control, Ecosystem balances, and Lindeman's 10% Energy law loop.

10th Maths (All Chapters)

CH 1 - CH 15 CORE MATHEMATICS FORMULAS
Ch 1: Real Numbers
HCF(a,b) × LCM(a,b) = a × b

Irrationality proofs ($\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}, \sqrt{5}$).

Ch 2: Polynomials

Quadratic format: $ax^2 + bx + c$. Zero relationships:

Sum (α+β) = -b/a | Product (α·β) = c/a
Ch 3: Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables

Intersecting ($a_1/a_2 \neq b_1/b_2$), Parallel ($a_1/a_2 = b_1/b_2 \neq c_1/c_2$), Coincident ($a_1/a_2 = b_1/b_2 = c_1/c_2$).

Ch 4: Quadratic Equations
Discriminant D = b² - 4ac | Roots: x = (-b ± √D) / 2a

D > 0: Real & Distinct, D = 0: Real & Equal, D < 0: No Real Roots.

Ch 5: Arithmetic Progressions (AP)
Nth term: an = a + (n-1)d | Sum: Sn = n/2 [2a + (n-1)d]
Ch 6: Triangles

Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT) criteria and AAA, SAS, SSS similarity conditions parameters.

Ch 7: Coordinate Geometry
Distance = √[(x₂-x1)² + (y₂-y1)²] | Section: [(mx₂+nx1)/(m+n) , (my₂+ny1)/(m+n)]
Ch 8: Introduction to Trigonometry
sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 | 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ | 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ

Values: $\sin 30^\circ = 1/2, \sin 45^\circ = 1/\sqrt{2}, \sin 60^\circ = \sqrt{3}/2$.

Ch 9: Some Applications of Trigonometry

Line of sight problems, Angle of Elevation and Angle of Depression matching equations using $\tan\theta = \text{Height}/\text{Base}$.

Ch 10: Circles

Tangent at any point is perpendicular to the radius. Lengths of tangents from external point are equal.

Ch 11: Constructions

Line segment inner divisions and tangents map drawing layout algorithms.

Ch 12: Areas Related to Circles
Area of Sector = (θ/360) × πr² | Length of Arc = (θ/360) × 2πr
Ch 13: Surface Areas and Volumes
Sphere Vol = (4/3)πr³ | Cone Vol = (1/3)πr²h | Cylinder Vol = πr²h
Ch 14: Statistics
Empirical Rule: 3 Median = Mode + 2 Mean

Mean calculation methods: Direct ($\sum f_ix_i / \sum f_i$), Assumed Mean setup.

Ch 15: Probability
P(E) = Favorable Outcomes / Total Outcomes | 0 ≤ P(E) ≤ 1

12th Biology (All Chapters)

🧬 BIOLOGY - FULL 13 CHAPTER PACK
Ch 1: Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants

Microsporogenesis, Megasporogenesis, Pollen-pistil interactions rules.

Double Fertilization = Syngamy + Triple Fusion (Forms 3N Endosperm)
Ch 2: Human Reproduction

Male/Female structures, Spermatogenesis loops vs Oogenesis breaks, Menstrual cycle hormones (LH surge, Progesterone), Blastocyst implantation.

Ch 3: Reproductive Health

Contraceptive tools (IUDs, Tubectomy, Vasectomy). STDs alerts. ART tools: IVF, ZIFT, GIFT setups.

Ch 4: Principles of Inheritance & Variation

Mendel's core laws. Non-Mendelian exceptions: Incomplete dominance, Co-dominance (ABO blood). Sex-linked defects: Hemophilia, Down's trisomy.

Ch 5: Molecular Basis of Inheritance
Central Dogma: DNA → Transcription → RNA → Translation → Protein

DNA semi-conservative replication, Lac Operon switches, DNA Fingerprinting sequences.

Ch 6: Evolution

Oparin-Haldane concept, Miller-Urey experiment spark values. Homologous vs Analogous parameters, Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium tracking.

Ch 7: Human Health & Disease

Malaria lifecycle paths, Typhoid/Amoebiasis vectors. Innate vs Acquired antibody systems, T/B cells loops, HIV/AIDS mechanism, Cancer tumor lines.

Ch 8: Microbes in Human Welfare

LAB curd setup, Saccharomyces bread making. Sewage biological oxygen demand (BOD) processing, Biogas methanogens, Biofertilizers tracking.

Ch 9: Biotechnology - Principles & Processes

rDNA tech rules. Restriction Endonucleases markers, pBR322 vector configurations, PCR cycles (Denaturation, Annealing, Extension).

Ch 10: Biotechnology & Its Applications

Bt Cotton toxic parameters, RNA interference (RNAi) shields. Humulin engineering, ADA gene therapy fixes, Transgenic safety validation models.

Ch 11: Organisms & Populations

Niche adaptations. Population curves (Logistic vs Exponential equations). Interaction models: Mutualism, Parasitism, Amensalism.

Ch 12: Ecosystem

Productivity layers (Primary/Secondary), Catabolism decomposition steps. Energy pyramids rules (Sea biomass inverted pyramid exceptions).

Ch 13: Biodiversity & Its Conservation

The Evil Quartet extinction accelerators. Conservation frameworks: In-situ (Biosphere zones) vs Ex-situ (Cryo banks, Zoo centers).
