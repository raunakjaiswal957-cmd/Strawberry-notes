Ch 1: Chemical Reactions & Equations

1. Chemical Equation (रासायनिक समीकरण):

Kisi chemical reaction ko symbols aur formulas ke roop mein likhna chemical equation kehlata hai.

Example: 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO (Magnesium Oxide)

2. Types of Reactions:

  • Combination Reaction: Do ya do se zyada reactants milkar ek single product banate hain.
  • Decomposition Reaction: Ek single reactant tootkar do ya do se zyada products banata hai.
Ch 2: Acids, Bases and Salts

1. Acids (अम्ल):

Swaad mein khatte hote hain aur Blue litmus ko Red kar dete hain. Ye H⁺ ions dete hain.

2. Bases (क्षारक):

Swaad mein kadwe hote hain aur Red litmus ko Blue kar dete hain. Ye OH⁻ ions dete hain.

3. pH Scale:

Acidic solutions ka pH 7 se kam hota hai, aur Basic solutions ka pH 7 se zyada hota hai. Pure water ka pH 7 hota hai.

Ch 3: Metals and Non-metals

1. Properties of Metals:

Metals chamakdar (lustrous), peetkar chadar banane layak (malleable), aur taar khinchne layak (ductile) hote hain. Ye electricity ke acche conductors hain.

2. Reactivity Series:

Metals ko unki reactivity ke ghatte kram mein rakhna. Potassium (K) sabse zyada reactive hai aur Gold (Au) sabse kam.

Ch 4: Carbon and its Compounds

1. Covalent Bond (सहसंयोजी आबंध):

Carbon hamesha electrons ki sharing karke bond banata hai, jise covalent bond kehte hain.

2. Hydrocarbons:

  • Saturated (Alkane): Single bond wale carbon compounds (CₙH₂ₙ₊₂).
  • Unsaturated (Alkene/Alkyne): Double ya triple bond wale compounds.
Ch 5: Life Processes (जैव प्रक्रम)

1. Nutrition (पोषण):

Autotrophic nutrition (plants jo apna khana khud banate hain) aur Heterotrophic nutrition (animals jo doosro par nirbhar hain).

2. Respiration (श्वसन):

Glucose ka tootkar energy (ATP) banana. Ye Oxygen ki presence (Aerobic) aur absence (Anaerobic) dono mein ho sakta hai.
