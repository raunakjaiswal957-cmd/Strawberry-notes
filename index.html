📚 My Study Notes

🔬 Science Notes

  • Photosynthesis: Plants use sunlight, water, and CO2 to make food (Glucose) and release Oxygen.
  • Newton's 1st Law: An object will remain at rest or in uniform motion unless acted upon by an external force (Law of Inertia).
  • Water Formula: The chemical formula for water is H2O, consisting of two Hydrogen atoms and one Oxygen atom.
  • Acid & Base: Acids taste sour and turn blue litmus red (pH < 7). Bases taste bitter and turn red litmus blue (pH > 7).

📐 Maths Formulas

  • Area of Circle: A = πr² (where r is the radius of the circle).
  • Algebra Identity: (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
  • Algebra Identity 2: (a - b)² = a² - 2ab + b²
  • Pythagoras Theorem: Hypotenuse² = Base² + Perpendicular² (a² + b² = c² for right-angled triangles).

💻 Coding & Web Dev

  • HTML Structure: HyperText Markup Language is the backbone of any website. It uses tags like <div>, <p>, and <h1>.
  • CSS Styling: Cascading Style Sheets are used to change colors, fonts, layouts, and make websites look beautiful.
  • JavaScript Variables: Used to store data. We can declare them using let (changeable) or const (fixed/constant).
  • Functions: A block of code designed to perform a particular task, executed when "something" invokes/calls it.
